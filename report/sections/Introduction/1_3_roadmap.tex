\subsection{ROADMAP}
\label{subsec:roadmap}

Section~\ref{sec:riskmeasures} defines the risk measures. Section~\ref{sec:model} presents the credit portfolio model, derives the conditional default probabilities, the portfolio loss and the scale of the asset returns, and establishes the invariance property that organises the rest of the analysis. Section~\ref{sec:data} describes the market and portfolio data and the stylised facts that motivate the three factor models, which are specified and estimated in Section~\ref{sec:factormodels}. Section~\ref{sec:simulation} sets out the simulation design and the calibration of the default thresholds. Section~\ref{sec:results} compares VaR and ES across the three models and measures their Monte Carlo error; Section~\ref{sec:rolling} follows them through time, Section~\ref{sec:irb} confronts them with the Basel IRB capital, and Section~\ref{sec:climate} adds a physical climate-risk overlay. Section~\ref{sec:conclusion} concludes. Appendix~A reviews Merton's model, Appendix~B details the Monte Carlo design and Appendix~C documents the code. The analysis was carried out within the HSG course on Quantitative Risk Management \citep{DeGiorgiFilkova26}; Appendix~D maps each item of the course brief to the section that addresses it and summarises what the paper adds to it.
