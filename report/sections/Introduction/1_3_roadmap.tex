\subsection{ROADMAP}
\label{subsec:roadmap}

The paper follows the order of the assignment \citep{DeGiorgiFilkova26}, and every section states the question it answers. Section~\ref{sec:riskmeasures} defines the risk measures. Section~\ref{sec:model} presents the credit portfolio model and derives the conditional default probabilities, the portfolio loss and the scaling of the asset returns, questions (i)--(iii). Section~\ref{sec:data} describes the market and portfolio data and the stylised facts that motivate our factor models, which are specified and estimated in Section~\ref{sec:factormodels}, question (iv). Section~\ref{sec:simulation} sets out the simulation design, questions (v)--(vii). Sections~\ref{sec:results} to~\ref{sec:climate} report the results: the comparison of the three models, question (viii), their evolution over time, question (ix), the regulatory benchmark, question (x), and the climate overlay, question (xi). Section~\ref{sec:conclusion} concludes. Appendix~A reviews Merton's model, Appendix~B gives details of the Monte Carlo design, and Appendix~C documents the code.
